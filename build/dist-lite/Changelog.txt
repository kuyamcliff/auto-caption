AutoCaption AE changelog

1.0.0

First release. Made by cyriqvfx.

- After Effects panel with a caption engine that runs fully on this computer.
- Transcribes the selected audio, video or precomp layer exactly as the
  composition plays it (start time, trims, stretch, time remap, nesting).
- Two quality levels: Fast and Accurate.
- Word-accurate timing for English, French, German, Spanish and Italian;
  every word shows where its timing came from (precise, estimated or edited).
- Caption editor: edit, split, merge, delete, caption and word timing, search,
  undo and redo, reset to the original transcript.
- Natural caption breaks and balanced two-line captions; changing words per
  line regroups instantly without transcribing again.
- 10 animation presets with a live preview at real word timings.
- Style editor and saved presets.
- Export JSON, SRT, VTT, ASS and TXT. Import JSON, SRT and VTT, and align
  imported subtitles to the audio.
- Engine check, file integrity check, diagnostics and logs.
- No telemetry, no analytics, no internet access.
