AUTOCAPTION AE 1.0.0 (LITE)
Made by cyriqvfx

This is the small download. It uses the Python already installed on your
PC instead of bringing its own, so it is about 360 MB instead of 4.3 GB.
It includes Fast quality and precise word timing for English. Accurate
quality and more languages are optional add-on packs (see below).

You need

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and After Effects 2022 (22.0) or newer
- 64-bit Python 3.11, 3.12 or 3.13 from python.org
- An internet connection once, for setup (about 1 GB download)
- About 5 GB of free disk space for setup (the engine environment is about 4 GB)

Getting started

1. Keep this folder somewhere permanent, for example C:\Tools\AutoCaptionAE.
2. Open the "AutoCaption Engine" folder and double-click "Set Up Engine.bat".
   It creates the engine's own private environment inside that folder and
   downloads the libraries it needs. Your Python installation and your
   other projects are not changed. This takes a few minutes, once.
   If you have an NVIDIA graphics card, setup offers to add graphics card
   support (about 2 GB more). Say no to keep it small; the processor works.
3. Double-click "Install AutoCaption.bat".
4. Open (or restart) After Effects, then Window > Extensions > AutoCaption AE.
5. Click "Check Engine". When everything is ready, click Start Captioning.
6. Select an audio, video or precomp layer and click Transcribe.

Add-on packs (optional)

- "Accurate quality.pak" (about 490 MB): adds Accurate quality.
- "More languages.pak" (about 760 MB): precise word timing for French,
  German, Spanish and Italian.
Put a pack file inside the "AutoCaption Engine" folder (next to engine.pak),
then click Check Engine in the panel. Without the packs, other languages
still transcribe with estimated word timing.

Good to know

- After setup everything runs on this computer. Your audio never leaves it.
- "AutoCaption Engine.exe" only runs when the panel starts it.
- Setup can be run again at any time to repair the engine.
- Moved the folder? Run "Set Up Engine.bat" again, then use the panel's
  Settings > Engine > Choose Engine Folder.

Need help?

Open Settings > Diagnostics in the panel and click "Copy diagnostics".
Licenses of components are in "AutoCaption Engine\Third-party notices.txt".
