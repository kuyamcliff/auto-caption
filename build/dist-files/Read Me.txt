Getting started

1. Keep this folder somewhere permanent, for example C:\Tools\AutoCaptionAE.
   The "AutoCaption Engine" folder must stay with it.
2. Double-click "Install AutoCaption.bat".
3. Open (or restart) After Effects.
4. Open Window > Extensions > AutoCaption AE.
5. The panel finds the engine folder by itself. If it asks, click
   "Choose Engine Folder" and pick the "AutoCaption Engine" folder.
6. Wait for the check to finish (about a minute the first time).
7. Select an audio, video or precomp layer and click Transcribe.
8. Edit the captions, pick words per line and an animation.
9. Click Create Text Layers.

Good to know

- Everything runs on this computer. Your audio never leaves it, and no
  account or internet connection is needed.
- Nothing else needs to be installed.
- "AutoCaption Engine.exe" only runs when the panel starts it. Opening it
  by hand just shows a short message.
- Moved the engine folder? Open the panel's Settings > Engine and click
  "Choose Engine Folder".
- A supported NVIDIA graphics card is used automatically. Without one,
  your processor is used.

Requirements

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), After Effects 2022 (22.0) or newer, about 5 GB
of free disk space and 8 GB of RAM (16 GB recommended for Accurate quality
on long recordings).

Need help?

Open Settings > Diagnostics in the panel and click "Copy diagnostics".
The licenses of bundled components are in
"AutoCaption Engine\Third-party notices.txt".
