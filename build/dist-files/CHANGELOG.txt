AutoCaption AE - Changelog
==========================

1.0.0
-----
First release.

- After Effects panel (CEP) with a local, offline caption engine.
- Transcribes the selected audio, video or precomp layer exactly as the
  composition plays it (start time, trims, stretch, time remap, nesting).
- WhisperX pipeline: pyannote VAD, faster-whisper (Base and Small models
  bundled), wav2vec2 forced alignment for English, French, German, Spanish
  and Italian, plus acoustic onset refinement of word starts.
- Every word carries its timing source: aligned / inferred / fallback / manual.
- Caption editor: edit, split, merge, delete, caption and word timing, search,
  undo/redo, reset to original transcription.
- Natural caption segmentation and two-line balancing; words-per-line changes
  regroup instantly without re-transcribing.
- 10 animation presets with a live preview at real word timings; word
  animations are built as AE expression selectors (one text layer per caption).
- Style editor and saved presets.
- Export JSON (full timing), SRT, VTT, ASS, TXT. Import JSON, SRT, VTT; align
  imported subtitles to audio.
- Backend self-test, SHA-256 integrity checks, diagnostics, rotating logs.
- No telemetry, no analytics, no network access at runtime.
